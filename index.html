How Stagience Works

Simple, powerful staging management in 4 easy steps

1

📦 Build Your Inventory

Add all your staging items to your digital inventory. Upload photos, add details like color, fabric, dimensions, and ID codes. Track where each item is located—warehouse, in transit, or at a project.

Pro tip: Use the bulk import feature or add items one-by-one with the guided wizard.
2

🏠 Create Projects

Set up each staging project with client details, property address, and project status. Stagience organizes projects by status—Pending, Scheduled, Active, Consult Only, or Completed—so you always know what's happening.

Pro tip: Projects automatically group by month/year, making it easy to find past work.
3

📅 Manage Timelines

Track every milestone with project timelines. Add consultation dates, installation days, pickup schedules, and more. Export timelines as PDFs to share with clients. Everything syncs with your calendar automatically.

Pro tip: Upload documents, photos, and files to each project for easy access.
4

✨ Work Smarter

With everything in one place, you'll save hours every week. Search and filter inventory instantly. Print professional reports. Organize photos by project. Use templates for common documents. Focus on staging, not admin work.

Pro tip: The mobile-responsive design means you can update inventory from anywhere.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Stagience

What is Stagience?

▼
Stagience is an all-in-one platform designed specifically for home staging professionals. It helps you manage inventory, track projects, organize photos, maintain client timelines, and store documents—all in one beautiful, easy-to-use interface. Built by a stager, for stagers.

How much does Stagience cost?

▶
Pricing details will be announced closer to launch. Join the waitlist to be notified first and receive special early-adopter pricing when we launch!

Can I access Stagience on my phone or tablet?

▶
Yes! Stagience is fully responsive and works beautifully on desktop, tablet, and mobile devices. Update your inventory from the warehouse, check project details on-site, or review timelines from anywhere.

How do I track my inventory?

▶
Add items to your digital inventory with photos, descriptions, colors, fabrics, dimensions, and ID codes. Mark items as IN (warehouse), OUT (on project), or HOLD. Search and filter instantly. Know exactly where every piece is at all times.

Can I organize photos by project?

▶
Absolutely! Each project has a Photos tab where you can create custom folders (Before Photos, After Photos, Pro Photos, etc.). Upload unlimited photos and organize them however you like. Perfect for keeping project documentation organized.

What about document storage?

▶
Each project has dedicated tabs for Documents (contracts, invoices, reports) and Files (floor plans, mood boards, checklists). Plus, upload and manage reusable templates for documents you use frequently. Everything stays organized by project.

Can I print inventory reports?

▶
Yes! Print professional inventory lists with photos, or export project timelines as PDFs. Perfect for sharing with team members, keeping physical records, or providing to clients.

When will Stagience launch?

▶
We're putting the finishing touches on Stagience now! Join the waitlist to be notified as soon as we launch. Waitlist members will receive priority access and special launch pricing.

Is my data secure?

▶
Yes! Your data is stored securely with industry-standard encryption. Only you have access to your account and information. We take data privacy seriously and will never share your information with third parties.

Can I import my existing inventory?

▶
Yes! We'll provide tools to help you import existing inventory from spreadsheets or other systems. Our team is also available to help with the migration process to make it as smooth as possible.
