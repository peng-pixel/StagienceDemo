📅

Schedule & Notes

Build a visual project timeline with consultations, staging days, photo shoots, and pickups. Add notes, team details, and access codes per event. Share a printable timeline with your client.

📄

Documents

Upload and store contracts, invoices, consultation reports, and any other project documents. Access them instantly from anywhere, no more hunting through email attachments.

🖼️

Photos

Organize photos into custom folders — Before, After, Pro Shots, and more. Browse in a beautiful fullscreen lightbox, select multiple photos to delete, and photos are compressed automatically on upload.

Files

Store floor plans, mood boards, staging guides, and any other files for the project. Download or share them anytime, all securely stored in the cloud.
