Everything you need to know about Stagience

Stagience is an all-in-one business management platform built specifically for home staging professionals. Manage your inventory, projects, calendar, photos, documents, and team — all in one place. Built by a stager, for stagers.
Stagience offers three plans: Starter at $29/month for solo stagers, Professional at $59/month for growing businesses with up to 3 team members, and Studio at $99/month for established companies with unlimited team members. Save 15% on all plans when you pay annually. Stagience is currently free during the beta period for all founding stagers.

Yes! Stagience is fully responsive and works on desktop, tablet, and mobile. Update your inventory from the warehouse, check project details on-site, or review your schedule from anywhere — no app download required.
Add items to your inventory with photos, descriptions, colours, fabrics, ID codes, purchase cost, and location. Mark items as IN (in the warehouse), OUT (on a project), or HOLD (reserved). Filter by status, category, or location — and bulk update multiple items at once. You can also print your inventory as a list or photo grid.
Yes! Stagience has a built-in CSV import wizard for both inventory and projects. Export your spreadsheet from Excel, Google Sheets, or Airtable as a CSV file, upload it, map your columns to Stagience fields, preview your data, and import in seconds. No manual re-entry needed.
Absolutely! Each project has a Photos tab where you can create custom folders — Before Photos, After Photos, Pro Photos, whatever works for you. Upload multiple photos at once, browse them in a lightbox, and bulk delete when needed. Photos are compressed automatically on upload to keep things fast.
Each project has dedicated tabs for Documents (contracts, reports, invoices) and Files (floor plans, mood boards, checklists). You can also upload reusable Document Templates — things like your standard staging contract or consultation form — and access them from anywhere.
