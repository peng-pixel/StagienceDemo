Our Story

Built by a stager,
for stagers.

Stagience was born out of a real problem — one that nearly every home stager faces every single day.

The problem we set out to solve

If you're a home stager, you already know the juggling act. Inventory tracked in a spreadsheet. Photos scattered across Dropbox or Google Drive. Project details in one app, scheduling in another.

It works — until it doesn't. A staging business has a lot of moving parts, and no single tool was ever built to handle all of them. So stagers cobble together a patchwork of apps and hope nothing falls through the cracks.

We knew there had to be a better way.

Why we built Stagience

After years of staging homes in Toronto, our founder Jen Punsalan was still juggling multiple apps to run her business. Inventory in one place, photos in another, projects tracked separately. It wasn't just inefficient — it was holding her business back.

She looked for a solution built specifically for home stagers. She couldn't find one. So she built it herself.

Stagience is the platform Jen wished she'd had from day one — one place for everything your staging business needs. Inventory tracking, project management, photo folders, team management, scheduling, and documents. All connected. All organized. All in one place.

No more app-switching. No more lost information. Just a calmer, more organized staging business.
